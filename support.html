FAQ

How is my protein target calculated?

Aviro estimates your protein target based on your profile, weight, and goal.

How is my workout plan created?

Aviro creates a simple weekly plan based on your goal, age, height, weight, and step baseline when available.

How do I connect Apple Health?

Open Aviro, go to Profile > Apple Health, and allow step access.

Is Aviro medical advice?

No. Aviro is a habit and planning tool only. It is not medical, fitness, or nutrition advice.

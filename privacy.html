DailyFit Privacy Policy

Simple, local, and in your control.

Your data stays simple

DailyFit uses only what it needs to personalize your plan.

No account Steps only Local data

Health data

  • DailyFit reads step count only after you allow Apple Health access.
  • Steps help personalize your walking baseline, step goal, and weekly guidance.
  • DailyFit does not read heart rate, sleep, workouts, medications, or clinical records.

Data on this device

  • Your goal, body profile, reminders, meal check-ins, and workout progress are stored locally.
  • DailyFit does not create an account or upload this app data to a DailyFit server.

Your control

  • Apple Health is optional.
  • You can change or remove Health access anytime in iOS Settings or the Apple Health app.
  • Deleting the app removes local DailyFit data from this device.

What DailyFit does not do

  • We do not sell your Health data.
  • We do not use Health data for advertising.
  • We do not share Health data with third parties.

Advisory boundary

  • DailyFit provides general habit guidance only.
  • It is not medical advice or a replacement for a qualified health professional.

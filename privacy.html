Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

Aviro Fitness is a habit and planning app designed to help you follow simple workouts, track meals and protein, view steps, and review weekly progress.

Information Aviro Fitness uses

Aviro Fitness may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Your selected fitness goal
  • Age, height, and weight
  • Meal selections, custom meals, and estimated protein progress
  • Workout and recovery completion status
  • Reminder preferences

Custom meals and protein tracking

When you add a custom meal, Aviro Fitness stores the meal name and protein amount locally on your device. Custom meals count toward your daily protein total and weekly protein progress.

Apple Health

Apple Health connection is optional. If you choose to connect Apple Health, Aviro Fitness reads your step count to show your steps and personalize movement guidance.

Aviro Fitness reads step count only. It does not read heart rate, sleep, workouts, medications, clinical records, or other sensitive health categories. Aviro Fitness does not write health data to Apple Health.

How your data is stored

Aviro Fitness stores app data locally on your device. Aviro Fitness does not create an account and does not upload your profile, meals, workouts, reminders, or progress data to an Aviro server.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Aviro Fitness may send local notifications for meal check-ins or planned workouts. Notification permission is optional and can be changed in iOS Settings.

Data sharing

Aviro Fitness does not sell your personal data. Aviro Fitness does not share your personal fitness or Health data with advertisers, third-party data brokers, or marketing platforms.

Your control

You can revoke Apple Health access at any time in iOS Settings or the Apple Health app. You can also turn notifications on or off in iOS Settings.

Deleting your data

Because Aviro Fitness stores app data locally, deleting the app removes local Aviro Fitness data from your device.

Medical disclaimer

Aviro Fitness provides general habit guidance only. It is not medical advice, fitness advice, nutrition advice, or a replacement for a qualified health professional.

FAQ

How is my protein target calculated?

Aviro Fitness estimates your protein target using your profile, weight, and selected goal. It is a simple planning estimate, not medical or nutrition advice.

Do custom meals count toward my protein?

Yes. Meals you add yourself count toward today’s protein total, the Today protein card, and weekly protein progress.

How is my workout plan created?

Aviro Fitness creates a simple weekly plan based on your goal, age, height, weight, and step baseline when available.

How do I connect Apple Health?

Open Aviro Fitness, go to Profile, then tap Apple Health. Aviro reads step count only after you allow access.

What Apple Health data does Aviro use?

Aviro Fitness uses step count only. It does not read heart rate, sleep, workouts, medications, or clinical records.

Where is my data stored?

Your goal, body profile, reminders, meal check-ins, custom meals, and workout progress are stored locally on your device.

How do I request data deletion?

Aviro Fitness stores app data locally. Deleting the app removes local Aviro data from your device. You can also email rexpcone@gmail.com for support.

Is Aviro Fitness medical advice?

No. Aviro Fitness supports habits and planning. It is not medical, fitness, or nutrition advice.

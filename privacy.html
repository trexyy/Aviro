Aviro Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Aviro is a fitness and wellness habit app designed to help you plan simple workouts, track meals, estimate protein targets, and view weekly progress.

Information Aviro Uses

Aviro may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Your selected fitness goal
  • Age, height, and weight
  • Meal selections and estimated protein progress
  • Workout and recovery completion status
  • Reminder preferences

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Aviro reads your step count to sync your steps and personalize your movement guidance.

Aviro does not read heart rate, sleep, medications, clinical records, or other sensitive health categories. Aviro does not write health data to Apple Health.

How Your Data Is Stored

Aviro stores app data locally on your device. Aviro does not create an account and does not upload your profile, meals, workouts, or progress data to an Aviro server.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Aviro may send local notifications for meal check-ins or planned workouts. Notification permission is optional and can be changed in iOS Settings.

Data Sharing

Aviro does not sell your personal data. Aviro does not share your personal fitness data with advertisers or third-party data brokers.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete Aviro from your device to remove local app data. You can also revoke Apple Health access at any time in iOS Settings or the Apple Health app.

Medical Disclaimer

Aviro provides general habit guidance only. It is not medical advice, nutrition advice, or a replacement for a qualified health professional.

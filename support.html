FAQ

How is my protein target calculated?

DailyFit estimates your protein target based on your profile and goal.

How is my workout plan created?

DailyFit creates a simple weekly plan based on your goal, age, height, and weight.

How do I connect Apple Health?

Go to Profile > Apple Health and allow step access.

Is DailyFit medical advice?

No. DailyFit is a habit and planning tool only. It is not medical, fitness, or nutrition advice.
